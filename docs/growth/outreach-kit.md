# ReelIntel outreach kit

Written 2026-11-27. Everything here is ready to send or paste. Each link
carries a `?ref=` tag, so sign-ups show which door they came through
(Supabase → Authentication → Users → a user → "User metadata" → `ref`).
That only counts web sign-ups. Someone who installs from the App Store
arrives with no tag.

Not done, on purpose: nothing below has been sent. These go out under
your name to people who will remember it, so you press send.

---

## 1. Alabama Saltwater Fishing Report podcast

Hosts: Butch Thierry and Joe Baya. Published by Great Days Outdoors.
Contact through the "Advertise with Us" page at greatdaysoutdoors.com;
I did not find a direct email address, so check the show notes.

**Subject:** Local angler built an app that answers "can I keep it?" offshore

> Joe, Butch,
>
> I fish out of Orange Beach and I listen to the report. I got tired of
> googling whether a fish was in season with one bar of signal, so I
> built an app that does it: take a photo, it names the fish and shows
> the season, size and bag limit for the water you are in. It works with
> no signal. It is called ReelIntel and it is free.
>
> Two things that might be useful to your listeners:
>
> 1. Alabama's private red snapper season closed November 16 and a lot
>    of people did not hear. The app tracks that kind of in-season
>    change against the state's own notices.
> 2. It grades each day for fishing using wind, seas and wave period at
>    the nearest offshore point, not the forecast for town.
>
> I would be glad to come on for ten minutes, or to sponsor a few
> episodes if that suits you better. Either way I would like your
> honest read on it: reelintel.ai/?ref=podcast

## 2. Charter captains (Orange Beach, Gulf Shores, Dauphin Island)

Over 100 charter boats work these waters. The pitch is what it does for
the captain: customers stop asking "what is this and can I keep it",
and every catch photo they share carries the trip.

**Subject:** Free cards for your boat — customers ID their own catch

> Captain,
>
> I am a local angler and I built a free app called ReelIntel. A
> customer takes a photo of their fish and it tells them the species,
> whether it is in season, and the size and bag limit. It works offshore
> with no signal.
>
> I would like to give you a stack of cards with a QR code for your
> boat. Your name goes on the card. There is nothing to pay and nothing
> to install on your end.
>
> If a customer lands a personal best, the app makes a share card they
> will post, and I am happy to put your boat's name on it.
>
> Can I drop some cards by the dock this week?
>
> Rob Boot
> ReelIntel · reelintel.ai/?ref=captain

The card itself is `docs/growth/captain-card.png`, sized 4 × 6 inches
at print resolution, with a blank "Compliments of" line.

## 6. App Store listing

Paste into App Store Connect → ReelIntel → the version page. The
subtitle limit is 30 characters and the keyword field 100.

**Subtitle (27 characters):**

```
Fish ID & Gulf Fishing Regs
```

**Keywords (98 characters):**

```
red snapper,season,bag limit,size limit,grouper,amberjack,offshore,saltwater,alabama,florida,tides
```

**Promotional text (shown above the description, can change any time):**

```
Is it open? Can I keep it? Snap a photo and get the species, season, size and bag limit for your waters. Works offshore with no signal.
```
